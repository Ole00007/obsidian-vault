Born on the waterfront

Where the harbor keeps its promises

Parruchiere sits a short walk from the fishing boats and the old warehouses of Porto Antico, where the sea breeze carries salt and the late-summer sun turns the stone warm as terracotta. We opened here on purpose: Genova's harbor has always been a place of arrivals, of people meeting, of love letters posted and honeymoons begun.

“Every flower we sell left the water once — now it returns to someone's hands by the sea.”

Our work is small and considered. We source from Ligurian growers when the season allows, condition each stem by hand, and arrange the way the light falls at six in the evening — soft, golden, a little in love.
