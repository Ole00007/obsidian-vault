# Multilingual Template Pack — sales pitch and client meeting summary

## Purpose

This document is a repeatable template pack for the initial client follow-up after a meeting. It combines a lighter messaging version, a premium version, a simple package architecture, and adaptation notes for different industries.

## Package Architecture

### 1. Democratic Economy

Best for small local businesses that need clarity, speed, affordability, and fast commercial impact.

**Communication style:** light, clear, practical.  
**Main focus:** leads, bookings, basic automation, reviews, local visibility.  
**Typical businesses:** beauty salons, barbershops, auto repair, small dental clinics, local clinics, family-run service businesses.

### 2. Middle-Up Premium

Best for businesses that need stronger positioning, premium service perception, brand value, a more advanced funnel, and a higher ticket.

**Communication style:** structured, premium, confidence-led.  
**Main focus:** digital infrastructure, CRM, service quality, omnichannel communication, reputation, controlled sales funnel.  
**Typical businesses:** law firms, audit and accounting firms, premium dental clinics, boutique clinics, premium auto services, engineering and automation firms.

## Industry Clusters

### Cluster A. Professional Services

**Who fits here:** law firms, audit, accounting, consulting.  
**Messaging logic:** trust, expertise, precision, confidentiality.  
**What to sell first:** website, lead forms, CRM, service structure, reputation marketing, SEO for expert-led queries.

### Cluster B. Technical & Industrial

**Who fits here:** vehicle automation, manufacturing automation, engineering companies, technical B2B firms.  
**Messaging logic:** competence, technology, reliability, efficiency.  
**What to sell first:** website, CRM, solutions catalogue, lead funnel, technical content, internal workflow automation.

### Cluster C. Local Appointment-Based Businesses

**Who fits here:** beauty salons, barbershops, dental clinics, car service / body shops, aesthetic studios.  
**Messaging logic:** easy booking, local search visibility, reviews, trust, fast communication.  
**What to sell first:** website, booking flow, Google Business, reviews, chatbot, CRM, local SEO.

### Cluster D. Local Service & Repair

**Who fits here:** car repair, repair shops, workshops, local service companies.  
**Messaging logic:** speed, clarity, trust, quality control, transparent customer journey.  
**What to sell first:** website, booking, CRM, status tracking, checklists, customer-touchpoint automation.

## Segment-Based Messaging Strategy

| Segment | Tone | Main emphasis | Sales angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Democratic Economy | Light, clear, direct | Fast results and accessibility | Sell through simplicity, value, and easy steps |
| Middle-Up Premium | Calm, structured, elevated | System, service, brand, scale | Sell through strategy, customer experience, and control |

## Template 1. Light Version

Thank you very much for the meeting 🙌😊  
Here is a quick summary of the main next steps for **[Business Name]**:

1. We create **[website / platform]** as the core digital base of the project.
2. We connect and structure **[CRM / booking / client database]** to improve speed, visibility, and internal efficiency.
3. We strengthen local visibility through **[Google Business / SEO / reviews / social media]**.
4. In phase one, we launch **[booking / request / consultation / reservation]** through a simple digital conversion flow.
5. In the next phase, we expand the system through **[app / chatbot / process automation]**.

## Template 2. Premium Version

Thank you for the productive meeting.  
Below is the preliminary structure of the collaboration for **[Business Name]**.

1. We build a central digital platform that becomes the foundation for the customer journey, communication, and future automation.
2. We design the CRM logic and consolidate the existing databases to improve control, process transparency, and operational quality.
3. We strengthen market presence through local and organic visibility, including **[Google Business / SEO / reputation / content structure]**.
4. In phase one, we activate the main conversion flow: **[booking / consultation / request / assessment]**.
5. In the next phase, we scale the ecosystem through **[app / chatbot / client area / advanced analytics]**.
6. In parallel, we establish quality standards, digital protocols, and control mechanisms to support long-term service growth.

## Recommended Use

- **After a first client meeting:** use Template 1 or 2.
- **For premium-facing clients:** use Template 2 by default.
- **For internal team alignment:** use Template 3.
- **For WhatsApp / Telegram:** keep the numbered structure and short paragraphs.
