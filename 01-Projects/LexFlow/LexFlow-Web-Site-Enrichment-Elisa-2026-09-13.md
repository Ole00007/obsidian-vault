## Language URLs (the hreflang decision)

Owner approved building real language URLs. The site previously switched EN/IT/RU
client-side from one URL, so a crawler only ever saw English and hreflang had
nothing legitimate to point at.

Built: `it/` and `ru/` folders with 16 pages whose HTML is genuinely Italian and
Russian, reciprocal hreflang (self + x-default) on all three variants of every
content page, per-language canonical/og:url, localised titles and meta
descriptions, and a language menu that navigates between URLs instead of only
swapping text. A page under `/it/` or `/ru/` pins its language.

Everything is generated by `templates/build-lang-sites.py` (also writes
sitemap.xml with 26 URLs and xhtml:link alternates), so it is reproducible rather
than hand-maintained.

`privacy.html` and `terms.html` have no translations, so no localised copies were
made and they are deliberately excluded from the hreflang set — a page in one
language needs no alternates, and claiming otherwise would be inaccurate.

Notable: the language switcher used to **destroy links** — `i18n.js` assigned
textContent to elements containing markup, deleting the Pricing link inside
`faq_a2` and the Privacy link inside `cookie_text`. Now only text nodes are
replaced.

## Why previous assistant work failed (evidence-based)

Three different things had been treated as one component:

1. The LP widget the brief points at (`#alWidget` / `#alLaunch`) is a **static preview** — its send button only un-hides a hardcoded "not sent in this preview" paragraph. There is no a-Lexy chat backend in the LP.
2. The real intake backend is the Flask `POST /submit`, which creates a matter and redirects to the token-secured `/status/<token>` page.
3. The assistant's **name lives in the AI orchestration layer** (Flowise), not the front end — so a text rename in HTML cannot change what the bot calls itself.

The name was genuinely 4-way in source: `Alessia`, `a-Lexy`, `aLexy`, `Lexy`. The old `alessia-widget.js` was a partial reimplementation, not a clone: it overwrote the greeting instead of appending bubbles, had no typing indicator, no conversation history, no focus trap, used urgency chips where the LP uses topic chips, pointed at `/api/chatbot` (an endpoint the LP never used) and had two real JS defects (duplicate `var pageI18n`; a `catch(err)` shadowing the outer error).

## Key open finding

The CRM schema cannot verify most of the pilot figures. There is **no billing or time-entry table** (so 2.4x billable activities has no instrumentation), no session/activity table (so 91% weekly active is unmeasurable), and no setup-duration or satisfaction instrumentation. The schema *can* verify matter cycle time and intake handling. Also: `app.py` exposes `/admin/load-demo` — if the pilot DB was seeded through it, every derived metric is demo data. See `verification-plan.md` in the repo.
