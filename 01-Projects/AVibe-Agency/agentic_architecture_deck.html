The Core Idea

One Brain, Few Tools, Full Control

Every request flows through a single orchestration brain (Hermes), routes to the cheapest capable AI model (OpenRouter), acts safely on your tools (Composio), and always lands back where your team and clients can see it (Airtable) — with private memory kept in Obsidian.
