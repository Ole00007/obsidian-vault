## 1. Strategic objective
Achieve separation **primarily by consensual/mediated path**, while **protecting the children** and
**neutralising Eugene's documented delay/guilt/leverage tactics** — preserving a credible judicial
fallback in plain sight (he knows it costs both parties).

## 2. Five-part strategy

### S1 — Lead with the mediation frame (he already wrote he will)
- He forwarded, in writing: **"I am willing to seek an amicable solution"** (24 Sep 2026, to Avv. Romanelli).
- Your five documented requests for mediator/civil settlement (Mar/May/Sep 2026) are on record.
- **Move:** the opening offer, sent through the avvocato, is a **formal mediation proposal** with a fixed
  meeting window. Whoever declines it is visibly the obstructionist — and we have screencap of his own
  "I will not fight" posture to hold him to.
- *Caveat (verified):* the "I will not fight / make it difficult" line is in the screenshots-OCR markdown,
  NOT the raw ZIP chats — **verify against the actual screenshot** before quoting it in an exhibit.

### S2 — Neutralise the "my house / get out" leverage (it's rented)
- He wrote "you take over the rent… or I cancel the contract" (21 Jun 2026). **The apartment is rented,
  not his.** His "I'm not leaving my house" is not an ownership claim over the family home.
- **Move:** put the **family-home assignment** (affido/collocazione) in the opening position tied to the
  **children's interest**, not to ownership — that's the correct Italian-law axis anyway (children follow
  the parent they live with).

### S3 — Three evidence-strong facts to anchor the settlement
1. **The split-houses plan was his too** — "we agreed within 4 to 5 months we should split houses"
   (27 May), "at the end of July we agreed to be separated" (21 Jun). Separation is joint, not only her idea.
2. **He offered amicable + the timeline himself** — same messages above; he cannot recast it as her unilateral exit.
3. **Repeated written mediator requests** (5 Mar, 27 May ×2, 24 Sep) — she is the party seeking cooperation,
   which is exactly the signal a judge/mediator weights for custody and tone.

### S4 — Pre-empt the counter-accusations (do NOT suppress them)
He will (and already did) claim: *she shouts, humiliates him in front of the children, films him and sends
it to his family, "many witnesses" see it's her.*
- **Move:** address these **proactively and calmly in the dossier** — acknowledge the shared-conflict dynamic,
  attach the recording-compliant use (art. 2712 c.c., defensive), and gently note **he never denied the
  Instagram fake accounts or reading the messages** in the session (only said "family photos"). A judge who
  sees both sides taken seriously cannot be blindsided by him producing the same chats.

### S5 — Financial lane kept separate & ledgered
He wrote he owes repayments (6k + 5k from Lessia + car insurance/rent offset) and that he "took all from my
company account — can't pay german taxes now."
- **Move:** a clean **loan/transfer ledger** with dates and amounts (who paid what, what's owed), kept out of
  the emotional lane. He proposed split; the ledger makes the split arithmetic defensible.

## 3. Sequencing (what the avvocato sends, in order)
1. **Opening letter of mediation** (avv.→avv.) with a fixed week-window and a 3-topic agenda (children,
   house, finances as **separate lanes**).
2. **Both-sides-answer-same-day clause** (kills his "you answer first" stall).
3. **Family-home proposal** tied to children, referencing his own 27 May / 21 Jun words.
4. **Ledger exhibit** (loans + offsets) with the **repaid-money** request.
5. Pre-emptive, **calm** paragraph acknowledging the dynamic + the verified "I will not fight" goodwill.
6. If he stalls past the window → the **judicial template is already staged** (cost shown to both).
