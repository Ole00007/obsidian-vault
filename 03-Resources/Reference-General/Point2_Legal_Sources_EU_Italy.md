# Point 2 — Legal Regulation Sources: EU & Italy (Extended Research)

## Direct Answer
For any EU/Italy legal-regulatory research, use a strict two-tier hierarchy: **Tier 1 (binding primary law)** from official government portals, and **Tier 2 (interpretive/secondary)** from reputable legal-research platforms. Never let Tier 2 stand in as the final citation for a binding rule.

## Tier 1 — Primary, Binding Sources

| Source | Jurisdiction | What it publishes | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **EUR-Lex** (eur-lex.europa.eu) | European Union | Official Journal of the EU, regulations, directives, consolidated texts, EU case law (CJEU) | The authoritative source for the exact legal text of any EU regulation — e.g. the full text of the AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) is published here as the Official Journal version[web:135][web:139] |
| **Normattiva** (normattiva.it) | Italy | Consolidated ("multivigente") Italian law, updated as amendments are enacted | Best for "what does the law say today," since it merges all amendments into one current text |
| **Gazzetta Ufficiale** (gazzettaufficiale.it) | Italy | Original enacted decrees and laws as first published | Best for tracing the exact original enactment date and wording before amendments |
| **Garante per la Protezione dei Dati Personali** (garanteprivacy.it) | Italy | Italy's GDPR supervisory authority — decisions, guidance, sanctions, official Italian DPA rulings | The binding enforcement-level source for Italian data-protection compliance questions[web:136][web:138][web:145] |

## Tier 2 — Secondary / Interpretive Sources

| Source | Coverage | Best Use |
|---|---|---|
| **vLex** (vlex.com) | 100+ jurisdictions, 1B+ documents, case law, AI-assisted research (Vincent AI) | Cross-jurisdiction comparison, case-law context, practitioner commentary |
| **Lexroom.ai** | Aggregated EU + national regulation and jurisprudence, AI-drafted summaries | Fast first-pass legal research — always verify against Tier 1 before treating as authoritative |
| **artificialintelligenceact.eu** | Secondary explainer site for the EU AI Act | Useful for plain-language summaries of AI Act obligations, but always cross-check the actual article text on EUR-Lex[web:140][web:141][web:143] |
| **GDPRhub.eu** | Community-maintained GDPR case database, including Italian Garante decisions | Good index of enforcement decisions, but treat as secondary — verify against garanteprivacy.it directly[web:145] |

## The Governing Rule
When answering a legal-regulatory question in any EU/Italy-scoped analysis:
1. State the binding rule first, citing EUR-Lex or Normattiva/Gazzetta Ufficiale/Garante directly.
2. Use vLex or Lexroom.ai only to add case-law context, comparative practice, or plain-language interpretation — clearly labeled as secondary.
3. If a regulator-specific authority exists for the sub-topic (e.g. Garante for privacy, Bank of Italy for financial services, ACI/PRA for vehicle registry), always add that specialized authority as an additional Tier-1 source alongside EUR-Lex/Normattiva.
4. Flag pending or recently enacted legislation explicitly — e.g. the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, in force since July 2024) is a live compliance driver that should be checked for applicability in almost any AI-adjacent business analysis[web:135][web:139].

## Extension Beyond EU/Italy
When a scoped analysis moves to a different country, replace Normattiva/Gazzetta Ufficiale with that country's official gazette/legislative portal, and replace Garante with the equivalent national regulator (e.g. a national data-protection authority, financial regulator, or sector-specific licensing body), while keeping vLex as a constant Tier-2 source since it covers 100+ jurisdictions from a single platform.
