## How OpenRouter Free Tier Works

- **No credit card required** to start — sign up with Google[^4]
- **Rate limits:** 20 requests/min, 50 requests/day free; jumps to **1,000/day** after a one-time \$10 top-up[^1]
- All free models use the `:free` suffix in the model ID — filter them at `openrouter.ai/models`[^4]
- Single API key works across all models — drop it into n8n, Make, or any OpenAI-compatible tool[^5]
