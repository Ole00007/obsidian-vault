# ICA v3 - 3: Legal Sources

Separate binding law from interpretive commentary; label citations by tier.

| Tier | Source | Coverage | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 EU | EUR-Lex | Official Journal, regs, directives, case law | Binding EU text |
| 1 National | Normattiva + Gazzetta Ufficiale (Italy) | Consolidated national law | Binding national law |
| 2 Aggregator | vLex (vlex.com) | 100+ jurisdictions, case law | Interpretation, comparison |
| 2 Aggregator | Lexroom.ai | Aggregated EU/national reg + jurisprudence | Fast first-pass, verify vs Tier 1 |

For non-EU/IT scope, substitute the equivalent official gazette. Never present Tier 2 as binding text - always trace back to Tier 1 for the actual requirement.

Cover: licenses, data-protection, sector regulation, pending changes - flag open risks.
