## 2. What the spouse can claim
| Asset | Italian rule | Claim |
|---|---|---|
| GmbH share | Prevailing view: a limited-liability holding falls into the community at once (art. 177(1)(a) c.c.). A minority view and a 2023 Cassation ruling treat it as deferred (art. 178). | Half the value of the share, as co-ownership or as a money credit. German company law decides who exercises shareholder rights. |
| Business set up after marriage | Deferred community (art. 178). Cass. S.U. 15889/2022: money credit; Cass. 21412/2026: 50% of value net of liabilities, fixed when the regime ends. | 50% of net value at the dissolution date. |
| Retained profit | Inside company equity. | Already in the share value. |
| Dividends and fruits | Art. 177(1)(b)-(c): fall into the community only if unspent at dissolution. | 50% of what remains unspent. |
| Eugene's pay and benefits | Same rule; also drives maintenance. | 50% of unspent savings; pay level feeds the allowance. |
| Shareholder loan (EUR 75,622.59 owed to BA-Teq at end 2024) | Eugene's personal debt to the company; raises book equity. | Do not net off automatically; ask what it financed and whether it is recoverable. |
| Hong Kong company | Same analysis if formed after the marriage. | Share value or 50% credit, plus tracing of revenue moved from BA-Teq. |
| Maintenance | Art. 5 L. 898/1970; Cass. S.U. 18287/2018: assistance plus compensation and equalisation. | Allowance for the spouse; child maintenance. Real income including retained profit is the key evidence. |

## 4. How to proceed
1. Evaluate the evidence with your trusted lawyer and, on his advice, fix the date on which the community will be dissolved. Valuation is as of that date.
2. Plead a declaration of what the community contains and a 50% division or credit (arts. 177, 178 and 194 c.c.).
3. Request disclosure: BA-Teq and BT Industries accounts, bank statements, shareholder ledger, loan documents, current register extracts. Counsel to confirm interim measures and asset-investigation powers.
4. Commission an expert valuation at the dissolution date, including goodwill and net of liabilities.
5. In any lump-sum settlement of the divorce allowance, reserve the community claim expressly (Cass. 4492/2021).
6. Plan enforcement abroad (section 5).

## 5. Enforcement abroad

| Matrimonial property | Reg. (EU) 2016/1103, Chapter IV (recognition, enforceability) | A declaration of enforceability is still needed |
| Maintenance | Reg. (EC) No 4/2009, Chapter IV | Enforceable without exequatur for states bound by the 2007 Hague Protocol |
Article numbers to be confirmed by counsel.

### Hong Kong
No EU regulation applies. Three routes exist:
- **Statutory registration.** The Foreign Judgments (Reciprocal Enforcement) Ordinance (Cap. 319) lets a creditor register a final and conclusive money judgment of a superior court within 6 years. Two law-firm articles list Italy among 15 designated jurisdictions under Cap. 319A; confirm against the Order itself. A declaratory community ruling is not a money judgment, so the Italian decision must fix a sum. Whether periodic maintenance and property orders qualify is for Hong Kong counsel.
- **Common law action.** If Cap. 319 does not apply, sue on the judgment as a debt. The Hong Kong Department of Justice describes the requirements: final, conclusive, competent court, fixed sum; defences include fraud, natural justice and public policy.
- **Fresh application in Hong Kong.** Part IIA of the Matrimonial Proceedings and Property Ordinance (Cap. 192) allows financial relief after a recognised overseas divorce, with leave and a Hong Kong connection (domicile, 3 years' residence or substantial connection). This is probably weak on Eugene's facts.
- **Practice case.** I found no Hong Kong decision enforcing an Italian matrimonial order. The common-law criteria were applied in JSC BTA Bank v Ablyazov, HCMP 341/2004 (CFI, 17 February 2014), a commercial case.
