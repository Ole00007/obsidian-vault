## Objectives achieved versus work still ahead

- **Achieved:** narrowed a broad “legal AI” idea to one testable promise: move an approved research finding into the correct operational matter once, then review a draft. Defined who selects the source, who drafts and who approves.
- **Achieved:** created a repeatable way to run a pilot: synthetic input, an independent variation, a stop condition and a comparison including review time. It can produce a useful “no” as well as a sale.
- **Partly achieved:** translated agency and product ambitions into reusable roles and briefs. What is not shown is that every role is connected, isolated, economical and working unattended.
- **Not established:** revenue attributable to the worker, a retained customer, measured client SEO gains, a sustained content cadence or a successful live installation. Treat each as an outcome to verify, not a founder-bio claim.

## Priority order I would use

1. **First customer, one decision.** Ask Tommaso where the research ends up and who re-enters it. If there is no handoff problem, stop that experiment. If there is, record one baseline, one independently repeated synthetic run and Tommaso’s decision.
2. **Commercial proof before expansion.** If the task saves time including review, propose a paid, fixed-scope pilot with one workflow, reviewer, acceptance criterion and price. Track offered / accepted / paid separately. If it does not save time, record why; do not compensate by adding agents.
3. **Stabilise only the path required for that pilot.** ADMY verifies build, matter boundaries and recovery; backy addresses one evidenced gap. Keep other hospitality, content and CRM opportunities in a backlog unless there is an existing client commitment or nearer revenue.
4. **Turn learning into distribution.** Make one consent-safe post from an observation, not from client details. Show one task before and after. Publish no performance claim until a real comparison supports it.
