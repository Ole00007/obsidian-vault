## Where the marketing automation / invoicing / AI claims actually hold up

Since you dropped the "no marketing automation, no invoicing, no ML" restriction, here's what's genuinely proven versus what's still marketing copy:

- **Odoo** is the most credible for invoicing because it's a full ERP suite — Accounting, Invoicing, and CRM are separate, mature apps used by millions of real companies, not bolted-on features.[^12][^19]
- **EspoCRM** and **SuiteCRM** both support workflow/BPM automation and marketing campaign modules, and independent reviewers confirm these work in production, though EspoCRM's advanced reporting/marketing depth is gated behind a paid "Advanced Pack".[^2][^29]
- **Vtiger** markets itself as "AI-Powered CRM" and has third-party validation (Gartner MQ inclusion, G2/Capterra Shortlist badges) that lends more credibility to its AI claims than a solo GitHub repo would.[^20]
- Nobody in this list has independently verified "ML lead scoring" claims the way OpenCRM advertised — if that specific feature matters, you'll need to test it live in the Odoo or Vtiger trial rather than trust a README.

## Practical next step for testing

Since you want to touch these before picking one: EspoCRM's no-registration demo and SuiteCRM's shared-login public demo let you poke around in under 2 minutes without any signup friction, while Odoo and Vtiger require an email but give you the full real product (not a locked demo) for 15–30 days. Given LexFlow's Flask/PostgreSQL stack, EspoCRM (PHP) and SuiteCRM (PHP) will require API-level integration rather than direct code merging, while Odoo's modularity may be overkill for an MVP — worth confirming feature depth firsthand before deciding integration strategy.[^4][^9][^23][^17]
