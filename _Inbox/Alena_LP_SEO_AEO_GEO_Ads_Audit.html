What CPC means

CPC = Cost Per Click. The amount charged each time someone clicks the ad. Views are free; clicks are paid. CPL (cost per lead) is roughly CPC divided by the conversion rate.

Abbrev.MeaningPlain explanation
CPCCost Per ClickWhat the advertiser pays each time someone clicks the ad (not when it is merely shown).
CTRClick-Through RateClicks / impressions. Shows how attractive the ad is.
CRConversion RateShare of clicks that become a call, WhatsApp message, booking click or form.
CPL / CPACost Per Lead / AcquisitionAd spend / number of leads. Approx. CPC / CR.
CPMCost Per MilleCost per 1,000 impressions (not our model - Search is paid per click).
GSCGoogle Search ConsoleFree source of truth for organic queries, indexing, CWV.
AEO / GEOAnswer / Generative Engine OptimizationMaking pages quotable by AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity.
NAPName, Address, PhoneMust be identical across site, GBP and directories.

Google Ads plan

ItemSetting / decision
Campaign typeSearch only (keywords). No Performance Max, no Demand Gen: health category restricts audiences and Demand Gen may not serve.
Targeting rulesUse keywords, location, language, predefined Google audiences. NO Customer Match, remarketing lists, lookalikes, audience expansion, custom segments.
Campaign 1 - Genova (ACTIVE)Landing: https://alenakrot.com/ . Location: Genova (presence). Language: Italian. Daily budget start 15 EUR.
Campaign 2 - Ventimiglia (PAUSED)Landing: https://alenakrot.com/medicina-estetica-ventimiglia . Languages: Italian, Russian, French. Daily 8 EUR when activated.
Campaign 3 - Palermo (PAUSED)Landing: https://alenakrot.com/medicina-estetica-palermo . Language: Italian. Daily 10 EUR when activated.
Ad group A - BrandExact/phrase: dottoressa alena krot, alena krot medico estetico, dott.ssa krot genova. Lowest CPC (brand ~0.25 EUR benchmark).
Ad group B - Category (decision for Massimo)Phrase: medico estetico genova, medicina estetica genova. Higher CPC, more reach. No treatment names in keywords during Phase 1.
Ad copy ruleBrand-led. No prices, discounts, treatment names, before/after, guarantees, superlatives ('il migliore'). Human review before publish.
Sample RSA headlines (<=30 chars)Dottoressa Alena Krot | Medico Estetico a Genova | Piazza Piccapietra, Genova | Prenota una Visita | Medico Chirurgo Estetico | Visita con la Dott.ssa Krot
Sample RSA descriptions (<=90 chars)Medico chirurgo specializzata in medicina preventiva ed estetica. Prenota una visita. | Socia SIME, AGORA e AITEB. Studio a Genova, Piazza Piccapietra. Contattaci oggi.
BiddingWeeks 1-3: Maximize Clicks with max-CPC cap 2.50 EUR (data collection). After 15-30 conversions: Maximize Conversions / target CPA ~25-30 EUR.
Welcome bonusSpend 400 EUR -> +400 EUR credited. Confirm exact window/terms in Billing > Promotions before launch; launch with budget that can reach 400 EUR inside that window (15 EUR/day ~ 27 days).
ConversionsPrimary: phone-call click, WhatsApp click, booking click (AlfaDocs/MioDottore), form. Secondary: scroll 75%, map click.
Review cadenceDay 3, day 7, then weekly: search-terms report, negatives, CPC vs forecast; escalate if CPC > 2x forecast.
Negatives (start list)gratis, lavoro, corso, scuola, stipendio, ecm, offerte, prezzi, costo, tariffe, usato, fai da te
Compliance: cosmetic procedures and injections sit in Google's sensitive Health category. Remarketing, Customer Match, lookalikes and audience expansion are barred; keywords, location and predefined audiences still work. Italian rules (legge 145/2018, FNOMCeO code) apply on top.
