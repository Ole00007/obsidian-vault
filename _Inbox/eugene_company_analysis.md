## 5. Findings
1. **Equity depends on the shareholder.** The shareholder receivable (EUR 75,622.59) exceeds 2024 equity (EUR 43,917.23). A full write-off would give negative equity of about EUR -31,705. This is a sensitivity test, not a finding of impairment.
2. **Equity fell by EUR 41,262.58 in 2023** and recovered by EUR 13,719.58 in 2024. Without a profit-and-loss account, losses, distributions and other movements cannot be told apart.
3. **The receivable grew by EUR 35,065 in 2023** while equity fell. This coincidence is a question for the ledger, not a conclusion.
4. **Liquidity is unclear.** Cash is not disclosed. The adjusted current ratio of 0.41 shows reliance on collecting the shareholder balance or other funding.
5. **Filing timing.** The 2023 accounts were adopted on 31 March 2025 and deposited on 29 April 2025. The 2024 accounts were adopted and deposited on 30 June 2026. The statutory disclosure period is 12 months after year end (section 325 HGB), so both look late. Confirm whether a fine was imposed.
6. **The Hong Kong invoice.** BT Industries billed EUR 1,890 (VAT 0%) for building a website for an Italian client, payable to a Hong Kong bank account. The business differs from BA-Teq's stated activity. A link to BA-Teq exists only through the filename and similar name. Whether the zero VAT treatment was correct is a tax question outside this research.

## 6. Why this matters for the spouse claim
- The share is valued from equity, so the shareholder receivable and goodwill drive the number.
- Illustrative floors at book value: half of 2024 equity is EUR 21,958.62; half of 2023 equity is EUR 15,098.83; half of the 2022 level is EUR 35,730.12.
- Revenue or clients routed to BT Industries would lower BA-Teq's value and raise the Hong Kong company's value. Both belong in the valuation.
