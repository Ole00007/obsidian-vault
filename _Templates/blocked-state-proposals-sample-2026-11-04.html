Design rationale. The single most important rule: blocked is a state, not a label — so it must change the card's shape (stripe, hatch, tint, dashed border), not just add text. Red = hard-blocked (legal/infra/override), amber = awaiting Ole. The pulse is reserved for cards awaiting your action specifically, so your eye goes straight to what only you can unblock. The blocked swimlane physically separates blocked work from actionable work. Reason is stored as a field (not prose) so the board can group/sort and the summary bar can count it.
